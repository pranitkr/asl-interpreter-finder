# 90-second demo script (5 beats)
Before recording: Staff page → "Reset demo data". Open the live URL on a phone-sized window, with a second window on the "My status" page.

**Beat 1 (0:00–0:15) A stressed requester types one sentence.**
"A hospital needs a DeafBlind-experienced interpreter in Tucson tomorrow. Today that means calling down a list. Here, I just type it."
Type: *Need a DeafBlind-experienced interpreter at Banner hospital in Tucson tomorrow at 2pm for about an hour.*

**Beat 2 (0:15–0:30) AI turns it into filters.**
"Workers AI reads it and fills the filters: Tucson, Medical, DeafBlind, tomorrow 2 PM, 1 hour. I can remove or change any chip. If AI is off, the same filters work by hand."

**Beat 3 (0:30–0:50) Broadcast to 3 interpreters.**
"Cards show who is available now, license date and contact by text or email, never just a phone call. I pick up to three and send one request."

**Beat 4 (0:50–1:10) An interpreter accepts, the requester sees it live.**
Switch to "My status", choose the matching interpreter, press Accept. Switch back.
"The first accept wins. The requester's page says Confirmed within 5 seconds. One tap writes a plain-language message for the Deaf client."

**Beat 5 (1:10–1:30) Staff dashboard shows the gap.**
Open Staff. "ACDHH staff see zero DeafBlind interpreters available in Yuma right now, a license expiring soon, and a request unfilled over 15 minutes in red. That is where to recruit and where to act."
Close: "Built on Workers, KV and Workers AI. Fully usable without AI, and fully keyboard accessible."
